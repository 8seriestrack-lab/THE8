This is the tier we actually match. Our pilot works out to ~$6.7K per car (9 cars, <$60K, found/prepped road cars, not bespoke chassis) — almost exactly Spec Miata's grassroots range, and far below BMW M2 or MX-5 Cup. JEVRA proves grassroots EV racing can run for 15+ years on this kind of budget. Electrathon proves the absolute floor exists and works. We're not inventing a cost structure — we're using one that's already been proven, repeatedly, for decades.

What We're Being Honest About

We don't have real revenue numbers yet. Nobody credible does, before a pilot. We had a version of this page with a five-year revenue projection on it. We took it down. It was guessed, dressed up to look calculated, and it wouldn't have survived the first serious diligence question.

What we actually have: quoted costs from the person who's going to run the pilot, not estimates from a template. And a researched set of comparable racing formats — what they cost to run, what they raised, and how long they took — so you can judge our numbers against reality instead of against our own confidence.

What changes after month 7: real attendance at the pilot, real cost-per-car, real sponsor interest (or lack of it), and real player data from the mobile and PC builds. That's when a revenue forecast stops being a guess and starts being a model. We'll build it then, with the same honesty.
