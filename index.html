The Problem

Gimmicky

Expensive

Hard to Monetize

We all know: it is getting harder to keep fans engaged.

The Solution

Priority Intersection Racing

A figure-8 urban circuit built around a priority intersection — the same infrastructure you navigate every day, reimagined as sport.

  • No straights long enough to get bored. No corners predictable enough to fall asleep.
  • Every lap, the intersection forces a decision. Yield or commit. Heroes and villains made in real time.
  • Works for sim racing and real events simultaneously.
  • AV / EV / ICE compatible — mixed fields create natural rivalries.
  • Modular arena deploys in any city, market-by-market.
By The Numbers

Format Specifications

Spec Why It Matters
15–20 vehicles / 2–3 categories Enough for chaos, few enough to follow. Categories create spread and natural narrative arcs on track.
2–3 km circuit length Compact enough for TV cameras to capture the whole track. Fans see the full battle, not just a segment.
Mixed field (EV / AV / ICE) Creates natural rivalries. Old tech vs. new tech. Purists vs. futurists. Drama baked into the entry list.
€15K–25K / season entry Accessible to emerging talent. Dramatically lower barrier than Formula E (€1M+) or comparable series.
The Result: Not a parade. Not a lottery. A high-speed chess match where the winner is the driver who best balances aggression, strategy, and nerve.
